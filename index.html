Introduction

Technology is important in education, communication and everyday life. Students use computers and mobile phones to research information, prepare assignments and communicate with teachers and classmates. The internet provides access to libraries, online lessons and educational videos that make learning flexible.

However, technology should be used responsibly. Students must evaluate information before trusting it, protect personal details and respect others when communicating online. Strong passwords, safe websites and careful sharing help reduce digital risks. Regular breaks also support healthy use of electronic devices.

Basic computer skills prepare students for further studies and employment. Word processing helps users create clear documents, while spreadsheets organise information and presentation software communicates ideas visually. HTML and CSS introduce learners to webpage structure and design. Practising these skills develops creativity, problem-solving and confidence. When technology is combined with discipline, curiosity and good judgement, it becomes a valuable tool for lifelong learning and participation in society.
